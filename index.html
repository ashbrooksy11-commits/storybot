STORYBOT

Short-form video creation

StoryBot is a desktop content-creation application for producing original narrated vertical videos.

TikTok integration

StoryBot allows an authorised user to upload a completed video to TikTok for review. The user completes editing and publishing within TikTok.

How StoryBot works

StoryBot combines story generation, locally generated narration, captions, gameplay footage and video composition into finished short-form videos. Users remain responsible for reviewing content and deciding whether to publish it.
