Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StoryBot handles information when users use the StoryBot application and its TikTok integration.

1. Information processed

StoryBot may process information that a user supplies to configure and operate the application, generated video content and metadata, and information required to connect an authorised TikTok account.

2. TikTok account information

When a user chooses to connect TikTok, StoryBot uses TikTok's authorisation process. TikTok may provide StoryBot with an access token, an account identifier and the scopes authorised by the user. StoryBot uses this information only to provide the requested TikTok integration.

3. Access tokens

TikTok access and refresh tokens are used to perform actions expressly authorised by the user. Users can disconnect TikTok from StoryBot to end the application's current connection.

4. Video uploads

When the user chooses to upload a video to TikTok, StoryBot transfers the selected video to TikTok. The user completes review and publication through TikTok.

5. Local processing

StoryBot performs core video-production tasks on the computer running StoryBot. Generated project files may be stored locally on that computer.

6. Third-party services

Features that interact with third-party services are also governed by the terms and privacy practices of those services.

7. Data sharing

StoryBot does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to a third-party service only as needed for a feature the user chooses to use, such as uploading content to TikTok.

8. User control

Users choose whether to connect TikTok and whether to upload content. Users can disconnect an authorised account from StoryBot.

9. Security

StoryBot is designed to limit use of authentication credentials to the functions for which the user authorised them. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

StoryBot is not directed to children and is intended for users who are eligible to use the connected services under their applicable terms.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated as StoryBot changes. The current version will be published on this page.
